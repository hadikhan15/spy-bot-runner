| 2026-10-03 | **Quant checks in every backtest ("Skill or luck?")**, ideas from professional backtesters: (1) **Monte Carlo permutation test**, 2,000 random runs: random trades on the same days, and random call/put at the model's own entry times; skill only if both beat random >95% of the time. (2) **Risk stats**: Sharpe, Sortino, worst and longest drawdown, profit factor, average win/loss, plus a drawdown chart. (3) **Which information helps**: each input group scrambled to see how much accuracy it carries. Tested: on random-noise data it reported "not clearly better than luck" even with a Sharpe of 1.87; with a planted edge it reported "looks like skill". |
